## Hosting and access data

This blog is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub processes the data needed to deliver the page. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. Where running the blog involves processing for which the blog operator is responsible, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interest is to provide the blog reliably and securely.

GitHub describes processing personal data in the United States and other locations. Its privacy statement explains data retention and the safeguards it uses for international transfers, including the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.

- [GitHub Pages on data collection](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection)
- [GitHub’s privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)

## Theme preferences

When you choose a theme, the blog stores its identifier in the session storage of your current browser tab. This keeps your chosen theme as you navigate. Session storage serves the display function you have selected, under section 25(2)(2) of the German TDDDG.

When you enable **“Remember theme on this device”**, the blog also stores the theme identifier and expiry date in your browser’s local storage. The preference is valid for up to 180 days from when it is saved. The entry is removed on your next visit after it expires. The legal basis is your consent under section 25(1) TDDDG. You can withdraw it at any time by clearing the checkbox in the theme menu. This deletes the persistent entry. The theme identifier and expiry date stay in your browser.

## External posts

GitHub Actions fetches external RSS feeds when it builds the blog. GitHub Pages serves the resulting cards. When you follow a link to an external article, the linked blog’s privacy notice applies to your visit.

## Contact and your rights

If you contact the blog operator, they process the information you provide to respond to your enquiry. The legal basis is generally the legitimate interest in responding under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. This information is kept for as long as it is needed to handle the enquiry or meet legal obligations.

Subject to the applicable conditions, you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability. Where processing relies on legitimate interests, you can object on grounds relating to your particular situation. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.
